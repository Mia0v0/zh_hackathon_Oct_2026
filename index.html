MONEY IN YOUR WALLET

Deliver, earn, spend

  1. When you complete a delivery and the requester confirms collection, its delivery fee is added to your wallet in AUD.
  2. When you post a request, choose Pay this request with earnings to use your wallet balance for its fee.
  3. Wallet money and credit points are separate: money pays request fees; points determine your rewards tier.
CREDIT POINTS

How points add up

  • On-time deliveries earn 0.5 points per estimated 100 metres, up to 5 points.
  • Late deliveries lose 0.5 points for each full 5 minutes late.
  • Completing an accessibility-priority delivery adds a 2-point bonus.

Points are not cash and can’t be spent from your wallet.
